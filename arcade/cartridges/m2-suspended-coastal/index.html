F1 How this works

This is a decision cartridge, not a puzzle with one right answer. Read the messages, pick a button (or press the number key shown, 1–4; press Enter for the highlighted choice, Esc to back out of a dialog). Every ping you send costs one of a budget of three — shown in the black readout above the buttons. A side panel tracks the Evidence and Assumptions boards. Nothing here is graded directly — at the end you can export a Markdown decision record shaped like the one you write for class.

F2 Step out of character

You can stop at any time, and stopping costs you nothing. Your grade is not affected. You do not need permission, and you do not have to explain.

Close this tab, or use the Canvas link at the left, whenever you want out. The cartridge keeps no record of leaving early.
