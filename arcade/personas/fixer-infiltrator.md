# The Infiltrator + The Fixer

Default Lead: Infiltrator. Default Handler: Fixer.

## Crossing

*The Introduction That Wasn't Free*

The Infiltrator needed a reference that would survive a deeper background check than
corporate onboarding usually runs — someone who had worked beside the cover identity, not
just filed its paperwork. The Fixer doesn't sell documents. Three days later, two former
AlgoCratic-Fujikara contractors were confirming a timeline over drinks on The Raft, telling
stories about a colleague they had never actually met. The Infiltrator never learned what
the Fixer paid them, or in what currency. Only that the reference held, and that
AlgoCratic-Fujikara's screening team moved on without a second look.

**The Cost**: The Fixer logged the favor as owed, not spent — and the favors the Fixer calls
in arrive on the Fixer's timeline, not whichever moment the Infiltrator can safely give.

**Aspect**: *"The Fixer Built My Cover Out of People Who Were Never There"*
Invoke: when you need social proof, a fabricated reference, or someone to vouch for an
identity that isn't fully real.
Compel: when the Fixer calls in the favor at the worst possible moment, or when one of the
invented witnesses surfaces somewhere unplanned.

## Opening exchange

- **Fixer (Handler)**: "You're covered. Three people owe me for this window alone."
- **Infiltrator (Lead)**: "Then let's not need a fourth."
- **Fixer**: "Fair. Channel's yours."

## Compel

**Trouble** (default Lead, Infiltrator): *"My Former Employer Wants Me Back or Dead"*

**Offer**: AlgoCratic-Fujikara pings a private channel mid-run — a debt forgiven or a
location leaked, depending how she answers, timed exactly when she's most exposed.

**Accept**: She reads the message, flags it, and keeps moving — filing away exactly what
they offered, letting them believe she might take it.

**Refuse**: She kills the channel without reading past the first line and spends a point to
make sure nothing about this run reaches them at all.

## Notes

- The three-line default Handler set (Fixer, transactional/warm) is **generic** — it does not
  reference this specific pair's crossing and is reused verbatim across all three Fixer-default
  pair sheets (`fixer-infiltrator`, `fixer-influencer`, `fixer-nomad`).
- The swapped one-line Handler set (Infiltrator, clipped/procedural) is likewise generic and
  reused wherever the Infiltrator ends up as Handler (`fixer-infiltrator`,
  `infiltrator-influencer`, both swapped).
- Only the **Opening exchange**, the **Crossing**, and the **Compel** are pair-specific.
- **Fixer palette proposal**: brass/gold `#C89B3C` against deep green `#0B3D2E`. The Fixer
  trades in favours and access, so a metallic gold-brass reads as currency and brokered
  value; the deep, ledger-green second tone sits apart hue-wise from all three existing
  duotones (orange/teal, magenta/navy, cyan/purple), so it does not collide with any of them,
  and it stays clear of the Influencer's actual gold accent by leaning duller and warmer.
