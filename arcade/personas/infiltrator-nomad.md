# The Infiltrator + The Nomad

Default Lead: Infiltrator. Default Handler: Nomad.

## Crossing

*Extraction Protocol Tango*

Faithful summary of the canonical crossing in
`solo-play/character/phase-trio.md` (Phase Two, Option A). Six months before this run, the
Infiltrator's first job against AlgoCratic-Fujikara went sideways — she got the data, but
security locked the facility down around her, twenty minutes from hard capture. She put out
the emergency call on the resistance channel. The Nomad answered without asking questions, no
negotiation on price: "Dock 7, pier side, ten minutes." They got her out with five minutes to
spare. She paid triple rate anyway — she knows what an immediate response is worth — and has
called the Nomad back twice more since. No questions, clean extraction, every time.

**The Cost** (as recorded in the source): Security footage shows the Nomad's boat.
AlgoCratic-Fujikara has it flagged. Every extraction after this one marks the Nomad hotter.

**Aspect** (as recorded in the source): *"The Nomad Gets Me Out When Things Go Wrong"*
Invoke: when you need extraction, emergency transportation, or maritime know-how.
Compel: when using the Nomad increases heat on both of you, or when the Nomad is unavailable
at the critical moment.

## Opening exchange

- **Nomad (Handler)**: "Dock's ready if this goes the way the last one did."
- **Infiltrator (Lead)**: "It won't. I've got five extra minutes this time."
- **Nomad**: "You said that last time too. Move."

## Compel

**Trouble** (default Lead, Infiltrator): *"My Former Employer Wants Me Back or Dead"*

**Offer**: The extraction channel she used against AlgoCratic-Fujikara gets pinged from the
other side — a better offer, routed through the one frequency only she and the Nomad still
monitor.

**Accept**: She answers just enough to learn what they know, letting them think the door
might still be open.

**Refuse**: She burns the frequency on the spot and spends a point, telling the Nomad to find
her a new one before the next run.

## Notes

- This is the one pair whose Crossing is not new material: it is a faithful compression of
  the canonical `solo-play/character/phase-trio.md` "Extraction Protocol Tango" crossing, with
  its **The Cost** and **Aspect** carried over rather than rewritten.
- The compel's offer/accept/refuse text is new, written specifically to hang off that
  crossing's extraction-channel detail — it is pair-specific, not generic.
- The three-line default Handler set (Nomad, laconic/maritime) is **generic**, reused
  verbatim in `influencer-nomad` (swapped) and `fixer-nomad` (swapped).
- The swapped one-line Handler set (Infiltrator, clipped/procedural) is likewise generic.
- No Fixer in this pair; see any Fixer pair sheet or `arcade/personas/README.md` for the
  palette proposal.
