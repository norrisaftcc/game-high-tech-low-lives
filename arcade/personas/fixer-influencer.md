# The Influencer + The Fixer

Default Lead: Influencer. Default Handler: Fixer.

## Crossing

*The Sponsor's Broker*

When Meridian Holdings' sponsorship contract needed a clause quietly rewritten before the
Influencer's legal team ever saw it, the Fixer was the name a producer whispered. The Fixer
didn't negotiate with Meridian directly — that would show up in a ledger somewhere. Instead
a "creative consultant" retainer appeared, paid to the Fixer's shell company, and the clause
that would have let Meridian claim ownership of every stream's raw footage quietly
disappeared before the signature page. The Influencer got a contract worth defending on
stream. Nobody at Meridian remembers asking for the change.

**The Cost**: The retainer is still on Meridian's books, filed under a name that isn't the
Fixer's — and whoever finds it will ask the Influencer first.

**Aspect**: *"The Fixer Edited My Contract Before Anyone Signed It"*
Invoke: when you need a favorable term buried in fine print, or someone who can move money
before a sponsor notices.
Compel: when Meridian's audit finally reaches that line item, or when the Fixer needs the
favor repaid on stream.

## Opening exchange

- **Fixer (Handler)**: "Sponsors are watching. So is everyone who owes me a favor tonight."
- **Influencer (Lead)**: "Good. Let them see something worth the retainer."
- **Fixer**: "That's the idea. Try not to make it cost me twice."

## Compel

**Trouble** (default Lead, Influencer): *"I Can't Turn Off — The Stream Is Always Running"*

**Offer**: Mid-run, chat begs for the angle she knows is reckless — narrating the sponsor
conflict live, on the record, because the numbers would be extraordinary.

**Accept**: She gives them the shot. It's reckless, it's true, and by the time the segment
ends, people who shouldn't have seen it, have.

**Refuse**: She cuts to a sponsor plug instead and spends a point to keep the real footage in
the vault where it belongs.

## Notes

- The three-line default Handler set (Fixer, transactional/warm) is **generic**, reused
  verbatim across all three Fixer-default pair sheets.
- The swapped one-line Handler set (Influencer, performing-to-audience) is likewise generic,
  reused wherever the Influencer ends up as Handler (`fixer-influencer`, `influencer-nomad`,
  both swapped).
- Only the **Opening exchange**, the **Crossing**, and the **Compel** are pair-specific.
- **Fixer palette proposal**: brass/gold `#C89B3C` against deep green `#0B3D2E`. See
  `arcade/personas/README.md` for the two-sentence rationale; the short version is that gold
  reads as brokered currency and the deep green stays clear of the other three duotones and of
  the Influencer's own gold accent.
