# The Infiltrator + The Influencer

Default Lead: Infiltrator. Default Handler: Influencer.

## Crossing

*The Feed That Almost Burned Her*

The Infiltrator was three minutes from a clean exit when the Influencer's drone caught her
face on a live stream of the same building, framed perfectly, timestamped, public. Forty
thousand people saw a woman who officially didn't exist. The Infiltrator didn't panic — she
waited for the comments to fill with theories, then let one specific rumor take hold:
industrial spy, corporate rival, anyone but who she actually was. The Influencer noticed the
deflection happen in real time, said nothing on stream, and never asked a follow-up question
either of them could survive answering.

**The Cost**: Forty thousand people have footage of the Infiltrator's face. It's buried under
a thousand other clips by now — but it never actually goes away, and the Influencer knows
exactly which file it's in.

**Aspect**: *"The Influencer Has Me on Camera and Hasn't Used It"*
Invoke: when you need the Influencer's silence, their footage, or a public distraction on
demand.
Compel: when someone else finds the clip, or when the Influencer's producer asks why it was
never posted.

## Opening exchange

- **Influencer (Handler)**: "We're live. Try to look less like someone who isn't supposed to
  exist."
- **Infiltrator (Lead)**: "Keep the drone off my face and we won't have a problem."
- **Influencer**: "No promises. Chat loves a mystery."

## Compel

**Trouble** (default Lead, Infiltrator): *"My Former Employer Wants Me Back or Dead"*

**Offer**: AlgoCratic-Fujikara recognizes a face in the Influencer's own audience analytics —
a security officer, watching the same stream she's currently exposed inside.

**Accept**: She stays in frame long enough to finish the job, betting the officer is watching
casually — and banking the exposure as a debt to settle later.

**Refuse**: She breaks from the shot immediately and spends a point, leaving the Influencer
to explain the sudden cut to forty thousand confused viewers.
