F1 · How this works

This is a decision cartridge, not a puzzle with one right answer. Read the messages, pick from the amber buttons (or press the number key shown, 1–4; press Enter to continue past a dice roll or a result screen). Some choices roll four Fate dice — each die shows +, 0, or − — added to a stat and compared against a difficulty. A side panel tracks any evidence or capacity boards the cartridge keeps; a one-line readout at the top tracks any budget (pings, points) in play. Nothing you do here is graded directly — at the end you can export a Markdown decision record shaped like the one you write for class.

F2 · Step out of character

You can stop at any time, and stopping costs you nothing. Your grade is not affected. You do not need permission, and you do not have to explain.

Close this tab, or use the Canvas link at the right, whenever you want out. The cartridge keeps no record of leaving early.
