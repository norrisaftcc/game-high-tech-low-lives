Planning and acting

Planning is free. Acting costs time.

A dashed choice is a plan: talk, look, think. It costs nothing. A solid choice is an act: it costs time and may roll dice. A double choice spends a Fate use.

Time and light

Each turn is ten minutes. Entering a new area takes a turn. Light burns down. In the dark you see nothing. A party that does not rest becomes tired.

Fights

Each member in turn gets an order: Fight, Ability, Item, Guard or Run. When all have one, the round plays. Reroll: spend Fate to roll the last miss again.

Cards and saves

Tap a card for abilities and marching order. The game saves in this browser after each step. The seed in the footer repeats the same dice. New game erases the save.
