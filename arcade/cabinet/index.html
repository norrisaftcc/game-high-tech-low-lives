How this works

Controls. TRACE/LOCK: touch near your air car to steer it; touch anywhere else on the field to aim and fire. DUAL VECTOR: left thumb moves, right thumb aims and fires. On a keyboard: arrow keys or WASD move, Space fires toward your last aim, and a mouse aims by hovering.

Two stakes profiles. Arcade keeps a hull bar and a score; you lose if the hull reaches zero. Classroom replaces the hull with a capacity board (To Do / In Progress / Blocked / Done) and a shifts counter that can rise and fall; damage blocks work instead of ending the run, and when everything is blocked your Lead's Trouble is compelled — accept it for a Fate point and a reset, or spend a Fate point to refuse.

What is recorded. Everything stays on this device, in this browser. A running decision log (stage results, complications, shifts, accuracy) can be exported at the end of a run as Markdown you copy or download yourself. Nothing is transmitted anywhere, and nothing here is graded.

F2 · Step out of character

You can stop at any time, and stopping costs you nothing. Your grade is not affected. You do not need permission, and you do not have to explain.

This is an optional demonstration. It is not course material, nothing here is graded, nothing is submitted, and nothing is transmitted anywhere. The game pauses while this panel is open.
