F1 How this works

This is a decision cartridge, not a puzzle with one right answer. Read the messages, pick a button (or press the number key shown, 1–4; press Enter for the highlighted choice, Esc to back out of a dialog). In the cargo window, tap an item (or press its number key, 1–8) to pack or unpack it — some items need another item packed first as an anchor, and the pack has a weight limit you can't exceed. Nothing here is graded directly — at the end you can export a Markdown decision record shaped like the one you write for class.

F2 Step out of character

You can stop at any time, and stopping costs you nothing. Your grade is not affected. You do not need permission, and you do not have to explain.

Close this tab, or use the Canvas link at the left, whenever you want out. The cartridge keeps no record of leaving early.
